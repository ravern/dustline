M9 sidearm

Adapted from textured pistol by Lotnik, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported. We joined the parts, adjusted orientation and scale, and converted the material while preserving the creator’s texture.

AK-family rifle

Adapted from AKM by LonesomeDucky, released under CC0 1.0. Orientation, scale, material setup, and texture size were adapted for the game.

Combat knife

Adapted from M9 Knife PBR by khairul169, released under CC0 1.0. Orientation, scale, materials, and texture size were adapted for the game.

Arms and original artwork

The arm surface is adapted from the MakeHuman Community base mesh under CC0, with new grips, gloves, proportions, and poses. The SCAR, Intervention, boots, and industrial props are original Dustline artwork.

Source links, licenses, and rebuild instructions accompany the artwork in the repository.
