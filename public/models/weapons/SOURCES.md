# Weapon assets

`ak47.glb` is adapted from **AKM** by **LonesomeDucky**, published under **CC0 1.0 Universal** on March 2, 2025. Original: https://opengameart.org/content/akm . License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Source: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/akm54.blend . Modifications: coordinate and scale normalization, rebuilt glTF material graph, and PBR textures reduced from 4096 to 1024 pixels. This model depicts an AKM, a member of the AK family.

`m9.glb` is adapted from **textured pistol** by **Lotnik**, published under **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported** on December 17, 2012. Original: https://opengameart.org/content/textured-pistol . License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/ . Source: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/pistol.zip . Modifications: joined components, coordinate and scale normalization, and conversion from Blender Internal materials to glTF metal/rough shading. The creator's diffuse texture remains at 1024 pixels. This model depicts a Beretta 92 style pistol, matching the M9 silhouette.

`knife.glb` is adapted from **M9 Knife PBR** by **khairul169**, published under **CC0 1.0 Universal** on March 14, 2018. Original: https://opengameart.org/content/m9-knife-pbr . License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Source: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/m9_knife.zip . Modifications: selected the low polygon M9-LP mesh, coordinate and scale normalization, rebuilt glTF PBR materials, and reduced the two texture sets from 2048 to 1024 pixels.

`intervention.glb` and `scar.glb` are original Dustline artwork generated by `tools/build-intervention.py` and `tools/build-scar.py`. They do not contain downloaded third-party gun meshes or textures. Their geometry and surface images are released under CC0 1.0 Universal: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ .


## Rebuilding downloaded assets

Download `akm54.blend`, `pistol.zip`, and `m9_knife.zip` using the source URLs above into a directory outside the public game bundle. Extract the pistol files into `pistol/` and the knife files into `m9_knife/`, preserving their adjacent image directories. The following command emits the three compact models and updates `stats.json`:

```sh
blender --background --factory-startup --disable-autoexec --python tools/build-weapons.py -- --source-dir /path/to/weapon-sources
```

Original models are rebuilt with Blender's `--python tools/build-intervention.py` and `--python tools/build-scar.py`. All exports use meters, Y up, and forward -Z. The source scripts retain the original material detail while applying a 1024-pixel texture budget. `stats.json` records the generated geometry, byte sizes, and bounds.

Keep this attribution file with distributed copies of the game, and link it from the game's credits so the CC-BY pistol attribution stays accessible.
